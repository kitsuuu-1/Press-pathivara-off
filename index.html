Frequently asked questions

What file format should I send?

PDF is best. We also accept Corel, Photoshop, and Illustrator files. If you only have an idea, we can design it for you.

Is there a minimum order?

It depends on the product. Contact us with your requirement and we will quote for small or large quantities.

How long does printing take?

Small jobs can be ready quickly. Larger jobs like books take longer. We confirm the date when you approve the proof.

Do you deliver?

Yes, we can arrange delivery. Ask us when you request a quote.
